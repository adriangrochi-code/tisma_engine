# A printer's camera (printer_connections.md, «Cámara USB de la computadora»,
# rules 1 and 5): a USB printer chooses «Sin cámara» or a USB camera in its
# panel; a network one below its data. With no camera connected (this
# machine has none) the list says so, and nothing is opened.
menu tab_device
expect-control camera_choice
expect-text No hay cámaras USB conectadas.
host octoprint http://127.0.0.1:9
wait 2
expect-control camera_choice
expect-text No hay cámaras USB conectadas.
