# Tisma — English listing

**Name** (30): Tisma: 3D slicer

**Subtitle** (App Store, 30): Slice and print from your phone

**Short description** (Google Play, 80): Slice your 3D models on your phone and send them to your printer.

**Keywords** (App Store, 100, comma separated): 3D printing,slicer,G-code,STL,3MF,Klipper,OctoPrint,Bambu,PrusaLink,filament,maker

**Description** (4000):

Tisma slices your 3D models on the phone itself, with the same engine as the desktop version, and sends them to
your printer over the network.

- Open STL, OBJ and 3MF from Files, from «Share with Tisma» or from Tisma's library.
- Choose your printer, nozzle and filament with the first-use assistant, from a catalog of free profiles.
- Tune the print at the Basic, Normal and Advanced levels, or with your own Custom view.
- See the model on the bed in 3D and the layer-by-layer preview, with the time, filament and cost.
- Send the G-code to Klipper (Moonraker), OctoPrint, PrusaLink, Duet or Bambu Lab in LAN mode, follow the print with
  the camera and get a notice when it finishes.
- Engineering: mark where the part is held and where it is loaded, and Tisma tells you whether it holds, with the
  safety map on the part and the lightest infill that still holds.
- Calibrate your printer with temperature, flow, retraction tests and more.
- Offline help: problems, procedures, glossary and material sheets.
- With a Tisma Cloud account, slice and analyze large projects in the cloud.

Your printers' access codes are kept encrypted on the phone and never leave it.

**What's new in the beta** (500): First beta: slicing on the phone, network printers, Engineering, calibration, the
profile pack and offline help. Tell us what you find in More › Report a problem.
