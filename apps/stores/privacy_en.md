# Tisma privacy policy (phone apps)

Draft for the beta; the owner reviews it and publishes it at a public address.

## What stays on the phone

- The models, G-code, settings and profiles you use.
- The printers you add and their keys or access codes: encrypted with the system's key store (the iPhone's
  Keychain, Android Keystore) and never sent anywhere.
- Printers are used only on your local network; the network search does not leave it.
- Print-finished notices are local: they go through no server.

## What leaves the phone, and only when you ask

- **Tisma Cloud** (if you sign in): your email and password when signing in (over HTTPS), the device's name
  («iPhone» or the Android model) for the list of sessions, and the files you send to be sliced or analyzed in the
  cloud with their results. The session is kept encrypted on the phone.
- **Purchases** (when available): the store's proof of purchase (App Store or Google Play), for the server to verify
  with the store and turn on your plan. Tisma never sees your payment details.
- **Report a problem:** what you write and, only if you accept it, the app's version, the system and the app's log
  (on Android). It is sent with your session when you are signed in.
- **Tisma's library:** when you search or download models, the phone asks our server for the index and the files; if
  you report a model, which one and why are sent.
- **Profile pack:** downloaded from our profiles repository; that download carries no data of yours.

## What we do not do

No ads, no usage statistics, no tracking across apps or sites, and no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
We do not ask for location, contacts, microphone or photos.
